## Week 1 — Project Introduction and Requirement Analysis

- Studied the MediBridge problem statement and identified the need for a unified healthcare workflow platform.
- Analysed the requirements of patient and doctor users.
- Identified the main modules: authentication, patient profiles, medical records, consent-based access, prescriptions, hospital discovery, appointments, emergency information, and financial assistance.

- Planned the overall architecture and separated the application into frontend, backend API, and clinical documentation service.
- Selected React and Vite for the client application.

- Identified Python, FastAPI, and Pydantic for the separate EvidenceScribe service.
- Reviewed supporting technologies and integrations, including Zustand, Axios, Tailwind CSS, Cloudinary, and external AI providers.
- Organized the repository into modular directories for easier development and maintenance.

**Outcome:** The technology stack and high-level architecture were defined.
